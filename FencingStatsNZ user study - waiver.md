FencingStatsNZ user study \- waiver

**What this is**  
I'm running a small study to find out whether fencers can read and understand what the site says about them. It's my own project, not a FeNZ study. The session is a 60-minute video call. You share your screen, work through a few short tasks on the site using your own results, and talk me through what you're thinking as you go. I'm testing the site, not you. There are no wrong answers, and getting stuck is the most useful thing that can happen.

You must be 18 or over to take part.

**Recording**  
With your permission, I'll record the session. If you'd rather not be recorded, that's okay. I'll take notes instead.

**Your rights**  
You can skip any task, take a break, or stop the session at any point, no reason needed. You can also withdraw afterwards: message me any time before the findings are published on the project's GitHub page, and I'll delete your recording and notes. Once the findings are published, they can't be pulled back, but they're anonymised as described below.

**What happens to the recording**  
It's stored in a private folder on my computer plus one backup. It's never uploaded to the GitHub repo or shared publicly. The sessions will be put into a third-party transcription software. One other person may watch two of the recordings to check that my scoring is consistent. Recordings are deleted within six months of the findings being published.

**How you'll appear in the write-up**  
As a code (P1, P2, and so on) with a rough description like weapon, level, and age category. Quotes will be anonymised. Your ratings on screen are already public on the site, but the write-up won't connect your code to anything that identifies you. One task asks you to guess the result between two fencers you know. These answers will not be linked to you in any way.  
**Future use**  
The anonymised findings may also be used in future academic work, possibly including a university thesis. Agreeing now covers that, so I won't come back later asking for permission you thought you'd already given.

**One thing to be aware of**  
The site might show you a rating that's lower than you'd expect. If anything you see bothers you, there's time at the end to talk about it, and you can stop at any point.

**Signing in**  
One task involves signing in to your profile with your FeNZ licence number. This is optional. If you'd rather not, I'll show the steps from a test account instead. Nothing gets submitted during the session. If you do sign in on screen, your licence number will be visible on the recording, which stays private as above.
